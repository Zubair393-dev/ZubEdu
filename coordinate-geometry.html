Chapter 7 • Coordinate Geometry

Important Question 1

Find the distance between the points (2, 3) and (8, 11).

Solution:

Distance = √[(8 − 2)² + (11 − 3)²]
= √[6² + 8²]
= √100

Answer: 10 units

Important Question 2

Find the midpoint of the line segment joining (2, 4) and (6, 8).

Solution:

Midpoint = ((2 + 6)/2, (4 + 8)/2)
= (8/2, 12/2)

Answer: (4, 6)

Important Question 3

Find the point which divides the line joining (2, 3) and (8, 9) internally in the ratio 1 : 2.

Solution:

Using section formula:
x = [1(8) + 2(2)] / 3 = 4
y = [1(9) + 2(3)] / 3 = 5

Answer: (4, 5)

Important Question 4

Find the distance of the point (6, 8) from the origin.

Solution:

Distance = √(6² + 8²)
= √(36 + 64)
= √100

Answer: 10 units

Important Question 5

Find the coordinates of the midpoint of A(−4, 6) and B(8, 2).

Solution:

Midpoint = ((−4 + 8)/2, (6 + 2)/2)
= (4/2, 8/2)

Answer: (2, 4)
