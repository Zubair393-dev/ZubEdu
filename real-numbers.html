Important Question 1

Use Euclid's Division Algorithm to find the HCF of 135 and 225.

Solution:
225 = 135 × 1 + 90
135 = 90 × 1 + 45
90 = 45 × 2 + 0

HCF = 45

Important Question 2

Find the HCF and LCM of 24 and 36 using prime factorisation.

24 = 2³ × 3
36 = 2² × 3²

HCF = 2² × 3 = 12
LCM = 2³ × 3² = 72

Important Question 3

Show that √5 is an irrational number.

Assume √5 is rational and write √5 = p/q, where p and q are coprime integers and q ≠ 0.

Squaring gives 5 = p²/q², so p² = 5q².
Therefore 5 divides p², so 5 divides p.
Let p = 5k. Then 25k² = 5q², giving q² = 5k².
Thus 5 also divides q, contradicting that p and q are coprime.

Therefore √5 is irrational.

Important Question 4

Find the prime factorisation of 156.

156 = 2 × 78
= 2 × 2 × 39
= 2² × 3 × 13

Answer: 2² × 3 × 13
