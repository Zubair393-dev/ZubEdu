Important Question 1

Find the mean of 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25.

Solution:

Mean = Sum of observations / Number of observations
= (5 + 10 + 15 + 20 + 25) / 5
= 75 / 5

Answer: 15

Important Question 2

Find the median of 7, 3, 9, 5 and 11.

Solution:

Arrange in ascending order:
3, 5, 7, 9, 11

There are 5 observations.
Median = middle observation

Answer: 7

Important Question 3

Find the mode of 2, 4, 4, 5, 6, 4, 7, 5.

Solution:

4 occurs 3 times.
5 occurs 2 times.
Other values occur once.

Answer: Mode = 4

Important Question 4

Find the mean of the following data using the assumed mean method: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50.

Solution:

Assumed mean A = 30.
Deviations are −20, −10, 0, 10, 20.
Sum of deviations = 0.

Mean = A + (Σd / n)
= 30 + 0/5

Answer: 30

Important Question 5

Find the median of the grouped data where the class intervals are 0–10, 10–20, 20–30, 30–40 and 40–50 with frequencies 5, 8, 12, 7 and 3.

Solution:

Total frequency N = 35.
N/2 = 17.5

Cumulative frequencies:
5, 13, 25, 32, 35

Median class = 20–30.

Using the median formula:
Median = l + [(N/2 − cf) / f] × h

= 20 + [(17.5 − 13)/12] × 10
≈ 23.75

Answer: Median ≈ 23.75
