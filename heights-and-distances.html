Important Question 1

From a point 20 m away from the foot of a tower, the angle of elevation of its top is 45°. Find the height of the tower.

Solution:

Let height of tower = h.
Distance = 20 m.

tan 45° = h / 20
1 = h / 20

Answer: Height = 20 m

Important Question 2

A pole 10 m high casts a shadow 10√3 m long. Find the angle of elevation of the sun.

Solution:

tan θ = Height / Shadow
= 10 / 10√3
= 1/√3

Therefore,
θ = 30°

Important Question 3

From the top of a 30 m high building, the angle of depression of a car on the ground is 30°. Find the distance of the car from the building.

Solution:

Angle of depression = angle of elevation = 30°.

tan 30° = 30 / distance
1/√3 = 30 / distance

Distance = 30√3 m

Answer: 30√3 m

Important Question 4

A ladder 10 m long makes an angle of 60° with the ground. Find the height reached by the ladder on the wall.

Solution:

sin 60° = Height / 10

√3/2 = Height / 10

Height = 5√3 m

Answer: 5√3 m
