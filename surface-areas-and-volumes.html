Chapter 12 • Surface Areas and Volumes

Important Question 1

Find the curved surface area of a cylinder with radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. Take π = 22/7.

Solution:

CSA = 2πrh
= 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 10
= 440 cm²

Answer: 440 cm²

Important Question 2

Find the volume of a cylinder with radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. Take π = 22/7.

Solution:

Volume = πr²h
= 22/7 × 7 × 7 × 10
= 1540 cm³

Answer: 1540 cm³

Important Question 3

Find the volume of a cone with radius 7 cm and height 24 cm. Take π = 22/7.

Solution:

Volume = 1/3 πr²h
= 1/3 × 22/7 × 7 × 7 × 24
= 1232 cm³

Answer: 1232 cm³

Important Question 4

Find the surface area of a sphere with radius 7 cm. Take π = 22/7.

Solution:

Surface area = 4πr²
= 4 × 22/7 × 7 × 7
= 616 cm²

Answer: 616 cm²

Important Question 5

Find the volume of a sphere with radius 3 cm. Take π = 22/7.

Solution:

Volume = 4/3 πr³
= 4/3 × 22/7 × 3 × 3 × 3
= 792/7 cm³
≈ 113.14 cm³

Answer: 792/7 cm³
