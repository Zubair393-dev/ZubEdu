Chapter 2 • Polynomials

Important Question 1

Find the zeroes of the quadratic polynomial x² − 5x + 6.

Solution:
x² − 5x + 6 = (x − 2)(x − 3)

Therefore,
x − 2 = 0 → x = 2
x − 3 = 0 → x = 3

Answer: Zeroes are 2 and 3.

Important Question 2

Find the zeroes of 2x² − 7x + 3.

Solution:
2x² − 7x + 3 = 2x² − 6x − x + 3
= 2x(x − 3) − 1(x − 3)
= (2x − 1)(x − 3)

Therefore,
2x − 1 = 0 → x = 1/2
x − 3 = 0 → x = 3

Answer: Zeroes are 1/2 and 3.

Important Question 3

If α and β are the zeroes of x² − 7x + 10, find α + β and αβ.

For ax² + bx + c:
Sum of zeroes = −b/a
Product of zeroes = c/a

Here a = 1, b = −7, c = 10.

α + β = −(−7)/1 = 7
αβ = 10/1 = 10

Important Question 4

Find a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are 4 and −3.

Solution:
For zeroes α and β, polynomial can be written as:
x² − (α + β)x + αβ

α = 4, β = −3
α + β = 1
αβ = −12

Polynomial = x² − x − 12

Answer: x² − x − 12

Important Question 5

Divide x³ − 3x² + 5x − 7 by x − 2.

Using polynomial division:

x³ − 3x² + 5x − 7 = (x − 2)(x² − x + 3) − 1

Quotient = x² − x + 3
Remainder = −1
