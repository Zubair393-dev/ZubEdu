Class 7 Mathematics - Integers

Q1. What are integers?

Answer: Integers are positive numbers, negative numbers and zero.

Q2. What is 5 + (-3)?

Answer: 2

Q3. What is 10 - 15?

Answer: -5

Q4. Which is an integer?
