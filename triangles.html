Chapter 6 • Triangles

Important Question 1

In two similar triangles, the corresponding sides are in the ratio 2 : 3. Find the ratio of their areas.

Solution:

Ratio of corresponding sides = 2 : 3
Ratio of areas = square of ratio of corresponding sides

= 2² : 3²
= 4 : 9

Answer: 4 : 9

Important Question 2

If ΔABC ~ ΔDEF and AB/DE = 3/5, find the ratio of their areas.

Solution:

For similar triangles,
Area(ABC) / Area(DEF) = (AB/DE)²

= (3/5)²
= 9/25

Answer: 9 : 25

Important Question 3

In a right triangle, the sides containing the right angle are 6 cm and 8 cm. Find the hypotenuse.

Solution:

By Pythagoras theorem,
Hypotenuse² = 6² + 8²
= 36 + 64
= 100

Hypotenuse = √100 = 10 cm

Answer: 10 cm

Important Question 4

In ΔABC, DE is parallel to BC. If AD = 3 cm, DB = 2 cm and AE = 4.5 cm, find EC.

Solution:

By Basic Proportionality Theorem,
AD/DB = AE/EC

3/2 = 4.5/EC
3EC = 9
EC = 3 cm

Answer: EC = 3 cm

Important Question 5

Check whether a triangle with sides 7 cm, 24 cm and 25 cm is a right triangle.

Solution:

Largest side = 25 cm

25² = 625
7² + 24² = 49 + 576 = 625

Since 25² = 7² + 24², the converse of Pythagoras theorem applies.

Answer: Yes, it is a right triangle.
