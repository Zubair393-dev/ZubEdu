Chapter 8 • Introduction to Trigonometry

Important Question 1

In a right-angled triangle, if the opposite side is 3 cm and the hypotenuse is 5 cm, find sin θ.

Solution:

sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse
= 3 / 5

Answer: sin θ = 3/5

Important Question 2

If cos θ = 4/5, find sin θ for an acute angle θ.

Solution:

Using sin²θ + cos²θ = 1

sin²θ + (4/5)² = 1
sin²θ + 16/25 = 1
sin²θ = 9/25
sin θ = 3/5

Answer: sin θ = 3/5
