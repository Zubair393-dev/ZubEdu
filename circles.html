Chapter 11 • Areas Related to Circles

Important Question 1

Find the area of a circle whose radius is 7 cm. Take π = 22/7.

Solution:

Area = πr²
= 22/7 × 7 × 7
= 154 cm²

Answer: 154 cm²

Important Question 2

Find the circumference of a circle with radius 14 cm. Take π = 22/7.

Solution:

Circumference = 2πr
= 2 × 22/7 × 14
= 88 cm

Answer: 88 cm

Important Question 3

Find the area of a sector of a circle with radius 14 cm and angle 90°. Take π = 22/7.

Solution:

Area of sector = θ/360 × πr²

= 90/360 × 22/7 × 14 × 14
= 154 cm²

Answer: 154 cm²

Important Question 4

Find the length of an arc of a circle with radius 21 cm and angle 60°. Take π = 22/7.

Solution:

Length of arc = θ/360 × 2πr

= 60/360 × 2 × 22/7 × 21
= 22 cm

Answer: 22 cm
